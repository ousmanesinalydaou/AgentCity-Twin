# Product overview

The workspace brings city layers, incidents, sensors, and modeled interventions together. Use **Overview** for system indicators and the natural-language command bar. Open **Digital Twin** to inspect the map, navigate layers, or choose a location. **Scenarios** stores repeatable inputs, while **Simulations**, **Reports**, and **Research** review results and experiments.

The public site is at `/welcome`. Use `/account` to sign in or create a workspace. Demo sessions are isolated and support running simulations and evaluating the operator review flow. Organization administrators have additional settings for account preferences, organization membership, agents, providers, model policy, and city data.
