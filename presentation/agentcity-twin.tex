\begin{frame}{How a scenario becomes a decision}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.67}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.24\linewidth}p{.69\linewidth}@{}}
\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{Describe}} & Set the location, start time, and severity.\\
\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{Simulate}} & Test the baseline and candidate interventions over five time points.\\
\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{Review}} & Specialists compare modeled outcomes against their own objectives.\\
\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{Compare}} & The network solver scores the options and any negotiated revision.\\
\textcolor{teal}{\textbf{Decide}} & An authorized operator reviews the evidence and can record a decision.\\
\end{tabular}
\note{1:50--2:50. Explain that the same modeled evidence can lead specialists to prefer different actions. The workspace weights combine their objectives in the ranking. The audit keeps a record of the process and makes the result available to an operator.}
